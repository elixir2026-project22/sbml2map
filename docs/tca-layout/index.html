These are earlier examples. The original published results remain unchanged.

The default is --tca-shape circle --exchange-alignment collinear. Choose --tca-shape square for a square TCA perimeter, or --exchange-alignment off to disable only exchange-triplet alignment.

TCA compounds and reactions follow their source order clockwise around the selected perimeter. Every other compound, reaction and cofactor copy stays outside, with clearance. Matched exchange triplets are ordered EX → extracellular → cytosolic from the outer boundary inward. Missing or ambiguous chemical matches are reported rather than guessed.
